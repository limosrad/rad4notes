| --- | --- |

| | Capillary telangiectasia |


## AVMs

:::differentials

| Feature | AVM | dAVF | 
| --- | --- |
| Aetiology | Congenital | Post-trauma or dural sinus thrombosis |
| Peak age | 20-40 years | 40-60 years |
| Location | Intraparenchymal | Skull base; dural sinus wall |
| Arterial feeders | Pial arteries | Dural arteries (e.g., meningeal arteries) |
| Intraparenchymal nidus | ++ | - |
| Flow-related aneurysm | 15% | rare |
| Haemorrhage risk | &uarr; (4% per year) | Variable (&uarr; if cortical vein involved)

:::trivia

:::trivia

Classification of dAVFs

| Pattern | Borden | Cognard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benign | I: Drains to dural sinus; no cortical venous reflux (CVR) | I: Drains to dural sinus without reflux; no CVR<br> IIa: Drains to dural sinus with reflux; no CVR |
| Aggressive | II: Drains to dural sinus; CVR present<br> III: Direct drainage to cortical veins or to isolated dural sinus segment | IIb: Drains to dural sinus, no sinus reflux; CVR present<br> III: Direct drainage to cortical veins; no venous ectasia <br> IV: Direct drainage to cortical veins + venous ectasia <br> V: Spinal perimedullary venous drainage |

:::
