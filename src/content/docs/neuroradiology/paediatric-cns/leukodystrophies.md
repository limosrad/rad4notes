## Normal and abnormal myelination

### Normal myelination

- T1 reaches near-adult pattern by 1 year
- T2 reaches near-adult pattern by 2 years

| Age (months) | T1 myelination | T2 myelination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | PLIC | PLIC |
| 6 | Corpus callosum (complete) | Splenium of corpus callosum |
| 12 | Complete | Corpus callosum (complete) and deep white matter |
| 18 | Complete | Near complete except juxta-cortical 'U fibres' and periventricular trigone  |
| 24 | Complete | Complete except at periventricular trigones |

- Progress of myelination is:
    - central &rarr; peripheral
    - caudal &rarr; rostral
    - dorsal &rarr; ventral
    - sensory &rarr; motor
