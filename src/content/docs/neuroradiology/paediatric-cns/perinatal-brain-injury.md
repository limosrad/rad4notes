## Germinal Matrix Haemorrhage

Germinal matrix haemorrhage:
- In preterm neonates; regresses by 36 weeks
- Timing: 1st within 3 days of birth, next at 2-4 weeks, then at term/discharge
- Grading:

| Grade | Feature | Frequency (%) | Mortality (%) | Morbidity (%) |
| --- | ---| ---| ---| ---| 
| 1 | Limited to caudothalamic groove | 40 | <12 | 15 |
| 2 | IVH without dilatation | 25 | <24 | 25 |
| 3 | IVH with dilatation | 20 | <36 | 50 |
| 4 | Periventricular haemorrhagic infarction | 15 | <48 | 75 |

## Hypoxic ischaemic encephalopathy

- Timing of MRI:
    - DWI is positive within 24 hours but underestimates severity within first 48 hours
        - Pseudonormalises by the end of 1 week
    - Best at 2-5 days; repeat at 10-14 days if clinical presentation discordant with early imaging

:::trivia

Sites of brain affected in hypoxic injury

| | Mild-moderate injury |	Severe hypoxia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preterm | Focal white matter injury &rarr; periventricular leukomalacia | Deep grey matter (especially thalamus) and brainstem (especially, anterior vermis and dorsal brainstem)<br>Basal ganglia and perirolandic cortex may be spared before 35-36 weeks due to poor myelination |
| Term | Peripheral (watershed) zones | Basal ganglia-thalamus pattern (putamina, ventrolateral thalami, hippocampi, dorsal brainstem, and lateral geniculate nuclei) and occasionally the perirolandic cortex |

:::
