## Pilocytic astrocytoma

- Circumscribed astrocytic glioma
- 5-15 years
- Cerebellar parenchyma (60%), optic pathways (25%), brainstem (midbrain, medulla), supratentorial (adult)
- Circumscribed, avidly enhancing, ↑ ADC, solid nodule with T2 hyperintense non-enhancing cyst. 
- Rare: mixed solid-cystic, or completely solid, enhancing cyst wall
- MRS: ↓Cr at 3 ppm
- WHO grade 1
- GFAP+, S100+, MAPK, BRAF alterations 

## Ependymomas

### Pathology
- Posterior fossa (60%):
    - Inferior part of 4th ventricle roof (PFB) - older children and adults
    - Foramen of Lushka (PFA) - 1 to 4 years of age
- Other: supratentorial intra-axial (30%), intramedullary spinal cord (10%)	

### Imaging features
- Relatively circumscribed enhancing tumour with ↑ ADC with toothpaste like plastic growth pattern through foramina of Magendie and Lushka
- PFA: encircle brainstem, Presence of ↓ADC may suggest anaplastic variant
- MRS: mI peak at 3.5 ppm
- WHO grade 2 and 3
    - PFA has poorer prognosis compared to PFB as difficult to completely resect


## Medulloblastomas

### Pathology
- Embryonal tumours
- median age of 6, most < 17 years
- Small round blue cell tumour, precursor cells – from brainstem (anterior vermis)

### Imaging characteristics

- Common location: superior part of 4th ventricle
- Heterogenous enhancing, infiltrative mass obliterating 4th ventricle, ↓↓ ADC, hydrocephalus, occasional peripheral cysts
- Leptomeningeal seeding in 25% (hence needs MRI spine)
- MRS: taurine peak at 3.35 ppm
- WHO grade 4

## ATRT
- Atypical teratoid/rhabdoid tumour (ATRT)
- Embryonal tumour	

### Pathology
- Median age 2-3 years, most < 4 years

### Imaging features
- Infratentorial and supratentorial
- Heterogenous or poorly enhancing, ↓↓ADC, large infiltrative tumours with peripheral T2↑ cysts
- Leptomeningeal seeding is common
- WHO grade 4, poor prognosis
