## GIST

- Most common mesenchymal origin neoplasm of the gastrointestinal tract (90%)

:::trivia
- IHC markers for GIST: CD117 (c-kit), PDGFR-A
    - Others: DOG1, CD34
- Paediatric GIST
    - Wild type CD117 and PDGFR-A
    - SDH-B deficient (Carney-Stratakis dyad)
:::

### Imaging differentials for GIST

:::differentials
- Differentials of submucosal lesions in GI

| Type | Enhancement | Size (cm) | Metastasis | Others |
| --- |--- |--- |--- |--- |
| GIST | Hypervascular<br>- homogenous when small<br>- heterogenous if large with necrosis | 1-15 | Liver, peritoneum, omentum | No lymph nodes |
| Carcinoid | Hypervascular<br>- homogeneous | <2 | Liver, lymph nodes | May be multiple, especially in hypergastrinemia |
| Glomus tumor | Hypervascular<br>- homogeneous | <5 | Rarely metastasizes | Predilection for gastric antrum |
| Schwannoma | Hypovascular<br>- homogeneous | <1-10 | Metastases are extremely rare | Homogeneous enhancement, even at larger size |
| Leiomyoma | Hypovascular<br>- homogeneous | 1–5 | None | Favors gastric cardia, length-width ratio of >1.2 |

:::

:::trivia
- Bull's eye lesions may also be seen with metastasis to stomach (from melanoma and breast cancer)
- Lymphadenopathy should prompt other diagnosis as it is almost never seen in GIST
:::

### Management
:::trivia
- Response assessment by Choi criteria.
    - Accounts for tumour liquefaction without obvious decrease in size seen with TKI agents.
:::

## Gastric adenocarcinoma

### CT in gastric adenocarcinoma
:::trivia
- Dual phase CT with late arterial phase and a portal venous phase is the workhorse imaging
    - PET/CT and MRI have slightly higher specificity with lower sensitivity &rarr; not routine
    - Nodal staging has low accuracy on imaging &rarr; all enlarged stations must be sampled
:::

### Differentials of malignant gastric neoplasms

:::differentials
| Feature               | Lymphoma      | Gastric carcinoma |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wall thickening |	Very thick|	Less thick|
|Perigastric fat planes|	Preserved	|Obliterated|
|Regional lymphadenopathy	|Large bulky	|Less bulky|
|Infrarenal nodes|	Possible	| Highly unlikely|
|Extension across pylorus|	Duodenum involved |Never crosses pylorus|
:::

### Staging of gastric adenocarcinoma

:::trivia
- Gastric cancer nodal disease

| Regional | 	Metastatic |
| --- | --- |
|D1: Paracardiac, curvatures, supra- and infrapyloric<br>D2: nodes around coeliac artery and its branches |	
|D3: hepatoduodenal*, posterior to pancreatic head, root of mesentery<br>D4: middle colic, para-aortic, retrocrural |
*12a (anterior hepatoduodenal) is part of D2
:::

- TNM staging

| T staging |	N and M staging |
| --- | --- |
| T1 – lamina propria and submucosa<br>T2 – muscularis<br>T3 – just before visceral peritoneum<br>T4 – visceral peritoneum &plusmn; surrounding structures | N1 - < 3 nodes<brN2 – 3-6 nodes<br>N3 - ≥ 7 nodes<br>M1 – distant metastasis |

- Stagewise treatment:
    - IA (T1N0M0): early gastric cancer - surgery with D1 lymphadenectomy
    - IB-III: locally advanced gastric cancer - perioperative chemotherapy + surgery with D2 lymphadenectomy
    - IV - metastatic : adjuvant therapy
