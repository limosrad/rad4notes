## Gastro-oesophageal hernia and volvulus

:::differentials
Gastro-oesophageal hernia:
| Feature | Type I | Type II | Type III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | Sliding | Paraoesophageal | Mixed |
| GE junction | above diaphragm | below diaphragm | above diaphragm | 
| Association | GERD | Incarceration | Incarceration, volvulus |

- Type IV: gastric + other visceral herniation
:::

:::

## Gastric folds

- Gastric fold thickening is >3–5 mm in the distal prepyloric stomach and >8–10 mm in the fundus:
    - Most common is alcoholic gastritis.
    - Most common neoplasm is lymphoma.
    - Inflammatory causes: Zollinger–Ellison syndrome, eosinophilic enteritis, Crohn disease, sarcoidosis, Helicobacter pylori, Menetrier disease

- Loss of gastric folds:
    - Atrophic gastritis: tubular and featureless narrow stomach
    - Linitis plastica: gastric wall thickening and rigidity
