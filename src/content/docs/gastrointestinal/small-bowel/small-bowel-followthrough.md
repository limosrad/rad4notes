### Stepwise approach to SBFT interpretation
Depends on the most prominent abnormality.
1. Abnormal folds:
    - Segmental or diffuse involvement?
    - Straight or nodular thickening?
    - Abnormal number?
    - Tethering or effacement?
2. Other supportive features:
    - Outpouchings?
    - Lesions?
        - Nodular? Cavitating? Polypoid? Annular?
    - Bowel dilatation?
    - Bowel separation?

## Abnormal bowel folds

### Segmental or diffuse? Straight or nodular?

:::differentials
| Fold thickening| Straight | Nodular |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Diffuse** <br> usually systemic| &darr; albumin (liver, heart or renal failure)<br>amyloidosis<br>abetalipoproteinemia<br>eosinophilic enteritis | Whipple's<br>Lymphangiectasia<br>Eosinophilic gastroenteritis<br>Mastocytosis<br>Amyloidosis<br>Waldenstrom<br>MAC in AIDS |
| **Segmental** <br> usually local | Ischaemia<br>Vasculitis<br>Radiation enteritis<br>Intramural haemorrhage| Crohn's<br>Lymphoma<br>TB<br>Infections |
:::

| Pathology | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| Whipple's | Bacillus and PAS+ macrophage accumulation &rarr; nodular thickening of jejunal > ileal folds<br>Other manifestations: hypoattenuating nodes, heart disease, arthritis, neurologic abnormalities |
| Mastocytosis | Mast cell accumulation &rarr; nodular thickening of folds<br>Other manifestations: urticaria, sclerotic bone lesions |
| Amyloidosis | Amyloid protein accumulation &rarr; nodular thickening of folds<br>Other manifestations: see [multisystem manifestations of amyloidosis](/front-matter/multi-system/)|

### Abnormal number?

| Pathology | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| Scleroderma | Smooth muscle atrophy and fibrosis &rarr; contraction<br>Oesophagus: patulous and dilated<br>Small bowel: &uarr; number of folds crowded in the distended jejunum with sacculation if asymmetric contraction ("hidebound" bowel) |
| Coeliac disease | Small bowel:&uarr; number of ileal folds relative to the jejunum (“jejunization” of ileum) (ﬂip-ﬂop pattern)<br>Duodenitis: (“bubbly” duodenum)<br>Others: hyposplenism, mesenteric and retroperitoneal adenopathy, cavitary mesenteric lymph node syndrome |

### Tethering? Effacement?

**Tethering**
| Pathology | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| Carcinoid | marked desmoplastic reaction &rarr; angulation and tethering of adjacent small bowel loops |
| Intraperitoneal metastasis | indistinguishable from carcinoid on SBFT |

**Effacement**
| Pathology | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| GvHD | obliterated folds &rarr; narrowed segment with smooth featureless appearance (ribbonlike, toothpaste, pipe stem, and lead pipe) |
| End-stage fibrosis | Any cause (Crohn's, radiation, ischaemia) |

## Other supportive features

### Outpouchings?
- Jejunal diverticulosis: small neck lesions
- Scleroderma: wide neck sacculations
    - Crohn's disease: pseudosacculations (opposite ulcer on mesenteric border)
- Meckel diverticulum

### Lesions?
- Polypoid
    - Mucosal lesions: adenomas, hamartomas, carcinomas
        - Muliple hamartomatous polyps in proximal small bowel &rarr; Peutz-Jaegers
        - Multiple sessile polyps in distal small bowel &rarr; carcinoid
    - Submucosal lesions: central ulceration ("bull's eye lesions") and obtuse angles to bowel wall
        - Single: benign mesenchymal lesion (lipoma, GIST, haemangioma, or neuroﬁbroma)
        - Multiple: metastasis (melanoma > breast and lung cancer), lymphoma, multiple carcinoids, Kaposi sarcoma
- Cavitated
    - Haematogenous metastasis: melanoma > lung
    - Lymphoma, Kaposi sarcoma, malignant GIST
- Annular: may cause obstruction
    - Adenocarcinoma
    - Metastasis
    - NSAID enteropathy: short weblike strictures
- Nodular: 
    - Nodular lymphoid hyperplasia: multiple, uniform, round, 1–3-mm nodules separated by normal mucosa in the terminal ileum
    - Lymphoma

### Bowel dilatation?
- Small bowel obstruction
- Adynamic ileus

### Bowel separation?
- Fibrofatty proliferation: Crohn's disease
- Displacement by a mass: lymphoma with mesenteric nodes, ascites
