## Pathology of oesophageal cancer

| | Squamous cell carcinoma | Adenocarcinoma |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | Upper two-third | Lower one-third |
| Risk factors | Tobacco<br> Alcohol<br> Achalasia<br> Plummer Vinson syndrome | Barrett oesophagus<br>Tobacco<br>Obesity |
| Incidence | Decreasing | Increasing |

## Imaging features of oesophageal cancer

### CT with oral neutral contrast

- Asymmetric irregular wall thickening
- Mediastinal invasion
    - Loss of intervening fat planes
    - Displacement or indentation of the trachea or other mediastinal structures
    - Vascular involvement suspected if &gt; 90 degree contact

## Differentials of oesophageal cancer

:::differentials
|       | Leiomyoma | Carcinoma |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin | Submucosal | Mucosal |
| Barium | Obtuse impression | Acute impression |
| Surface | Smooth or lobulated | Irregular |
| Mediastinal invasion | - | + |
| FDG PET | Minimal uptake | Avid uptake |
:::

## Oesophageal cancer staging

### Imaging modalities for staging oesophageal cancer
:::trivia
- Choice of imaging for T staging: Endoscopic ultrasound
- Choice of imaging for N and M staging: PET/CT
:::

### TNM staging for oesophageal cancer

| T staging | N and M staging |
| --- | --- |   
| T1 – lamina propria and submucosa<br>T2 – muscularis<br>T3 – adventitia<br>T4 – surrounding structures<br>- T4a – resectable (pleura, pericardium, azygos vein, diaphragm, or peritoneum)<br>- T4b – unresectable (aorta, vertebra, trachea) | N1 - < 3 nodes<br>N2 – 3-6 nodes<br>N3 - ≥ 7 nodes<br><br>M1 – distant metastasis |

### Regional nodes for oesophageal cancer
- supraclavicular
- cervical
- mediastinal
- perigastric
- coeliac
