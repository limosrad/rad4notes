:::differentials
Corpus luteum vs ectopic pregnancy

| Ectopic pregnancy | Corpus luteum |
| --- | --- |
| RI < 0.4 or RI > 0.7 | RI 0.4-0.7 |
| Thick echogenic rim | Thin echogenic rim |
| “Ring of fire” | “Ring of fire” |
| Moves separate from ovary (mc site – ampulla of fallopian tube) | Moves with ovary |
:::
