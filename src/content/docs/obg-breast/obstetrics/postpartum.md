## Postpregnancy haemorrhage (PPH)

| Primary | Secondary |
| --- | --- |
| &lt; 24 hours | &gt; 24 hours to 12 weeks |
| Atony (most common)<br>RPOC<br>Lower genital tract trauma<br>Abnormal placentation<br>Coagulopathies |  Placental site subinvolution<br>RPOC<br>Endo(myo)metritis<br>GTD<br>Uterine vascular anomalies |

### Enhanced myometrial vascularity (EMV)

- Low resistance flow in involuting vessels in immediate postpartum period
- Doppler USG: &gt; 20 cm with low resistance flow
- May persist and cause blood loss in presence of RPOC

### Retained products of conception (RPOC)

- Persistent chorionic villi after evacuation
- USG: endometrial thickening &gt; 10 mm or mass distending endometrial cavity
- Beta HCG &uarr;: possible, especially in early pregnancy loss
- Grading:

    | Type | Vascularity compared to myometrium |
    | --- | --- |
    | 0 | nil |
    | 1 | &darr; |
    | 2 | = |
    | 3 | &uarr; |

Hypervascular findings in postpregnancy period

| Pathologic Finding | Prior Pregnancy? | Prevalence | Origin | hCG | Key Diagnostic Information | Management |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EMV | + | Common | Myometrial | mildly &uarr; | Centered in the myometrium | Conservative or intervention |
| Type 3 RPOC | + | Common | Endometrial and myometrial | mildly &uarr; | Centered in the endometrium | Conservative or intervention |
| Placental subinvolution | + | Rare, but possibly under-recognized | Myometrial | - | Histologic diagnosis | Not well established |
| Uterine AVM | - | Extremely rare | Myometrial | - | Congenital and extremely rare | Conservative or intervention |
| Uterine artery pseudoaneurysm | + | Rare | Myometrial and/or endometrial | - | To-and-fro pattern Doppler pattern | Intervention |
| Arteriovenous fistula | No | Unknown | Myometrial | - | Single, direct arteriovenous connection | Intervention |

:::

- In older literature, EMV = AVM.
    - AVM is congenital malformation, not related to pregnancy.
