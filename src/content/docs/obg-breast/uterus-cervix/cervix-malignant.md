## Cervical cancer staging:

- I – limited to cervix
    - IA - <2 cm
    - IB – 2-4 cm
    - IC - >4 cm
- II – extension to upper 2/3 vagina [IIA] / parametrium [IIB]
    - IIA1 - < 4 cm
    - IIA2 - > 4 cm
- III – extension to lower 1/3 vagina / pelvic side walls ± hydronephrosis / nodal disease
- IV – other pelvic organs / distal metastasis

## Cervical cancer treatment

- I, IIA1 → radical surgery
- IC, II-IV → chemoradiation
