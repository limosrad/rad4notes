## Endometrial cancer staging:

- I – myometrial invasion
    - IA - <50% myometrial invasion
    - IB - > 50% myometrial invasion
    - IC (new): LVSI / aggressive type; without myometrial invasion
- IIA – cervix
    - IIB/IIC (new): LVSI / aggressive type; + myometrial invasion
- III – uterine serosa / vaginal or parametrial / nodal disease
- IV – other pelvic organs / distal metastasis

## Endometrial cancer treatment

- staging laparotomy + transabdominal hysterectomy + bilateral oophorectomy + LNectomy ± adjuvant
