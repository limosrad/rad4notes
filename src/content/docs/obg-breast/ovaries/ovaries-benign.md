## Ovarian cyst management

- Post-menopausal (RCOG guideline 34, 2025):
    - < 3 cm – no follow-up
    - &gt; 3 cm – measure CA-125 and calculate RMI
        - ≥ 200: CT abdomen and pelvis → MDT
        - < 200: 
            - Asymptomatic, simple, unilateral, unilocular, 3-5 cm: repeat in 4-6 months
            - Any not present: consider surgery (BSO)
- Pre-menopausal (RCOG G62, 2011 and BMUS 2025):

| Cyst type | Cyst size | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple cyst |	<30 mm | Physiological → no follow-up |
| Simple cyst | 30–49 mm | Almost certainly benign → no follow-up |
| Simple cyst | 50–70 mm | US follow-up at 1 year |
| Simple cyst |	>70 mm	| Consider MRI + surgical assessment |
