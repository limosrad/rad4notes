---
title: Surgery for Congenital Heart Disease
description: Common patterns on chest radiograph in congenital heart disease
---

Neonatal cardiac surgery for hypoplastic left heart:

1. Norwood: To allow systemic circulation within days of birth
    - Increase LV → RV by increasing ASD size and a BT shunt (R-Scl → RPA)
    - Increase calibre of aorta
    - Complications: RPA aneurysms, SVC syndrome, shunt insertion stenosis

2. Bidirectional Glenn: Partial cavopulmonary circulation at 3-6 months of birth
    - End-to-side connection of SVC→RPA
    - Closure of BT shunt

3. Fontan: Total cavopulmonary circulation; systemic circulation by RV alone at 1.5-5 y
    - Conduit between IVC → RPA bypassing heart entirely
    - Complications: RA enlargement, plastic bronchitis
