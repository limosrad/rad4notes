## Blunt traumatic thoracic aortic injury

- Isthmus is the most common site

### Types

| Grade | Finding | Management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | intimal tear, intimal flap, or both | Non-operative |
| 2 | intramural hematoma | Non-operative<br>TEVAR if high-risk |
| 3 | disruption with pseudoaneurysm | TEVAR |
| 4 | disruption with free rupture | TEVAR > open |

### High-risk features

- Posterior mediastinal hematoma >10 mm
- Lesion to normal aortic diameter ratio >1.48
- Mediastinal hematoma causing mass effect
- Pseudocoarctation of the aorta
- Large left hemothorax
- Ascending aortic, aortic arch, or great vessel involvement
- Aortic arch hematoma
