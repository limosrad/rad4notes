## Effects of radiation on tissues

- Direct effects → ionisation of DNA, RNA and protein
    - Primary cause of damage from high linear energy transfer (LET) radiation (α- and neutron particles)
- Indirect effects → free radical formation which causes cellular and DNA damage
    - Most common is H2O → H2O2 production as water is most common molecule exposed to radiation
    - Primary cause of damage from low LET radiation (x-rays and γ-rays)

## Deterministic vs stochastic effects of radiation

| Tissue reactions = deterministic effects |	Stochastic effects |
| --- | --- |
| Severity increases with dose of radiation | Probability of effect occurring increases with dose |
| Probability of injury is unrelated to dose | Severity is not related to dose of radiation |
| Examples: skin erythema, fibrosis, haematopoetic tissue damage | Examples: radiation induced cancer, hereditary effects of radiation |
| Effects happen when dose crosses a threshold, which has individual variability | No dose threshold below which risk is zero |
| Do not follow LNT model, which described probability of occurrence without a threshold | Linear no threshold model: risk increases with any dose → theoretical basis of ALARA principle |
| Unlikely to be seen in diagnostic imaging (rarely: in prolonged fluoroscopy) | Principle health risk from low-dose radiation (e.g., diagnostic radiation) |
| Occur within days to years of exposure | May occur years after exposure |


### Specific tissue effects to radiation:

- Effects are most pronounced in mitotic (M) and gap / pre-mitotic (G2) phases.

| Tissue |	Effect |	Radiation absorbed dose (Gy) |	Time to effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skin | Initial erythema | 2  | 2-24 hours |
|  | Erythema | 3-6 | 1-4 weeks |
|  | Epilation | 3-4 | 2-3 weeks |
| Lens of eye | Cataract | 3-5 | Years |
| Bone marrow | Depression | 0.5 | 3-7 days |
| Testis | Temporary sterility | 0.1 | 3-9 weeks |
| Ovaries | Permanent sterility | 3 | < 1 week |

- Acute whole-body exposures:

| Whole body dose (Gy)	| Principal affected organ | Time to death (days) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-6 | Bone marrow  | 3-60 |
| 5-15 | GIT and lungs | 10-25 |
| &gt;15 | CNS | 1-5 |
