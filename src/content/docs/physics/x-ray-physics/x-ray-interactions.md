---
title: Interactions of X-rays with Matter
description: Photoelectric, Compton effects
---

Possible interactions of x-rays with matter:
1. Photoelectric effect
2. Compton scatter (inelastic scatter)
3. Rayleigh scatter (elastic scatter, coherent scatter, classical scattering)
4. Pair production
    - At diagnostic energy levels (25-150 kVp), only the first two are significant.

## Photoelectric effect

- Photon + inner shell electron → complete absorption + ejected photoelectron + characteristic radiation or Auger electron emission
    - Since there is complete absorption of initial photon, photoelectric effect produces attenuation without scatter

- Probability of photoelectric effect occurring is 
    $$
    \propto \rho \cdot \frac{Z^3}{E^3}
    $$

### Effect on diagnostic images

- Bones have higher Z compared to soft tissue → greater absorption of x-rays
- ↓ photoelectric effect at high kVps → ↓ x-ray absorption at high kVps → ↓ tissue contrast at high kVps

### K-edge effect

- Photoelectric effect cannot occur at below a certain binding energy
- When mean beam energy crosses this energy (called k-edge), there is sudden increase in photon attenuation due to photoelectric effect
- For iodine, k-edge is at 33.2 kV
    - Hence mean x-ray energy of diagnostic imaging with contrast is just above this energy level.
        - The mean energy is approximately \frac{kVp}{3}$


## Compton effect

- Photon + outer shell electron → Lower energy photon + ejected electron
- The low energy photon will be emitted at an angle dependent on the initial photon energy.

- The probability of Compton scatter at diagnostic energy range:
    $$
    \propto \frac{\rho}{E}
    $$

### Effect

- ↑ Compton effect occurs at low kVps → ↑ noise at low kVps

## Comparative effects on image quality

- &darr; kVp: both photoelectric effect &ge; Compton scatter occur
- &uarr; kVp: beyond k-edge, there is rapid &darr; in photoelectric effect compared to Compton scatter &rarr; less noisy but poor contrast images

### Choice of kVp in different imaging modalities

- Mammography at 25-34 kVp as soft tissue (↓Z) has maximum photoelectric effect at low kVps
- Radiography at 60-80 kVp as bone shows relatively good photoelectric effect at slightly higher kVs too
- Chest x-ray at 125 kVp to decrease photoelectric attenuation by ribs at higher kVp
