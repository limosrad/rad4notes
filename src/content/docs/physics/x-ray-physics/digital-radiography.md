## Methods of signal detection

| CR |	Indirect DR	| Direct DR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photons absorbed by phosphor crystal within cassette<br>↓<br>High energy photoelectrons released<br>↓<br>Ionisation of atoms in path, with trapping of electrons in various crystal sites<br>↓<br>Red laser readout releases electrons with emission of blue light<br>↓<br>Light converted to current by a photomultiplier tube | Photon hits a CsI:Tl scintillation layer, releasing green light<br>↓<br>Detected by active layer of a-Si:H, separated into pixels containing a PD, CCD and TFT switch<br>↓<br>PD: amplifies signal<br>CCD: stores signal of latent current<br>TFT switch: during readout converts charge to electric current | Photon hits a photoconducting device (a-Se), creating holes and electrons<br>↓<br>Capacitor draws in all the positive potential (holes)<br>↓<br>TFT switch: during readout converts charge to electric current |
