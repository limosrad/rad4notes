## Otitis media

:::differentials
Middle ear hypoenhancing (on T1 C+) masses:

| Finding | Congenital cholesteatoma = epidermoid | Pars flaccida acquired cholesteatoma | Pars tensa acquired cholesteatoma | Cholesterol granuloma |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | ↓= | ↓ | ↓ | ↑ |
| T2 | =↑ | ↑ | ↑ | ↑ |
| DWI | ↑ | ↑ | ↑ | - |
| Location | TM intact, anteroinferior | Superior Prussak’s space | Inferiorsinus tympani | Smooth mass in middle ear |
| Ossicular displacement | Lateral | Medial displacement | Lateral displacement | Depends on location |
:::

## Temporal bone trauma

:::differentials

| Temporal bone fracture | Longitudinal | Transverse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Predominant involvement | Middle ear | Inner ear |
| Frequency | 80% | 20% |
| Fracture line | Parallel to long axis (RL)| Perpendicular to long axis (AP)|
| Labyrinth | Spared | Involved: vertigo, sensorineural hearing loss |
| Ossicles | Involved: conductive hearing loss | |
| Tympanic membrane | Involved | Spared |
| Facial paralysis | 20% | 50% |
