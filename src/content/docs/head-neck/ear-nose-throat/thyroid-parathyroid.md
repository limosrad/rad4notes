## ACR TIRADS

### Composition

(Choose 1)

| Description | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Cystic or almost completely cystic | 0 |
| Spongiform | 0  |
| Mixed cystic and solid | 1  |
| Solid or almost completely solid | 2 |

- Spongiform: Composed predominantly (>50%) of small cystic spaces. Do not add further points for other categories.
- Mixed cystic and solid: Assign points for predominant solid component.
- Assign 2 points if composition cannot be determined because of calcification.

### TIRADS scoring and clinical significance

| 0 Points | 2 Points | 3 Points | 4 to 6 Points | 7 Points or More |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TR1 | TR2 | TR3 | TR4 | TR5 |
| Benign | Not Suspicious | Mildly Suspicious | Moderately Suspicious | Highly Suspicious |
| No FNA | No FNA | FNA if ≥ 2.5 cm<br>Follow if ≥ 1.5 cm | FNA if ≥ 1.5 cm<br>Follow if ≥ 1 cm | FNA if ≥ 1 cm<br>Follow if ≥ 0.5 cm* |

- \*No FNA or follow up for TR-5 lesions < 0.5 cm as they are likely to be indolent.
- Up to two most suspicious lesions should undergo FNAC
- Lesions showing threshold growth (↑ by 20% and 2 mm in 2 dimensions or ↑ in volume by 50%) without meeting FNAC criteria → surveillance at 1 year.
