## Approach to focal liver lesions

|  | Adenoma | FNH | Haemangioma | HCC | FLC |

| Sex | F > M | F > M | F > M | M > F | M = F |
| Capsule | Yes | No | No | Thin | Thin |
| Central scar | Uncommon | Yes, T2 &uarr;| No | Uncommon | Yes, T1 and T2 &darr; |
| Calcification | - | - | &plusmn; | 7% | 50% |
| Enhancement | Uniform arterial | Uniform arterial &rarr; delayed scar enhancement | Centripetal | Arterial (may be mosaic) &rarr; porto-venous washout | Uniform arterial &rarr; there is no scar enhancement |
| Colloid scintigraphy | Reduced uptake | Normal uptake | Reduced uptake | Reduced uptake in less well-differentiated tumours | Reduced uptake |
