## Approach to focal liver lesions

- Cirrhotic liver: use LI-RADS for classification
- Non-cirrhotic liver: can classify lesions based on some specific characteristics

![Focal liver lesions in non-cirrhotic patients](/images/flowcharts/liver-focal-lesions-non-corrhotic.svg)

## Cystic liver lesions

- USG: anechoic with posterior acoustic enhancement &plusmn; septations
- CT: attenuation 0-20 HU with no obvious enhancement (septae may enhance)
- MRI: T1 and T2 signal follows CSF
    - Occasional T1 &uarr; due to protein content may occur

| Unilocular thin-walled liver cysts | Multilocular or thick-walled liver cysts |
| --- | --- |

## Special cases

### T1 hyperintense lesions

- Melanoma metastasis
- Haemorrhage due to any cause
- Non-crystallised calcium

### T2 hypointense lesions

- Usually benign
- Exception: mass forming cholangiocarcinoma
    - heterogenous, restricted diffusion, IHBRD, &uarr; CA 19-9

## Arterial phase hyperenhancing lesions

- Persistence: APHE remains hypernhancing to background liver on portal phase
- Washout: APHE becomes hypoenhancing to background liver on portal phase
- Fade: APHE becomes isoenhancing to background liver on portal phase
