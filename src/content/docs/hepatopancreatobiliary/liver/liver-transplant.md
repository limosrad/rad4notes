## Liver transplant complications

Hepatic artery RI in post-transplant period:

| ↑ RI (> 0.8) | ↓ RI (< 0.5) |
| --- | --- |
| Transient rise: preserved diastolic flow, resolves by 4 days | Hepatic artery stenosis |
| Impending thrombosis (flow absent in thrombosis) | Thrombosis with collaterals |
| Splenic steal | Coeliac artery stenosis | 
| Graft rejection or dysfunction | Arterioportal fistula |
