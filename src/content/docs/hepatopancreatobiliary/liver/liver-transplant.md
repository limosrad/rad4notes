## Pre-transplant workup

- Aims: donor safety and surgical planning

### Modalities

| | CT | MRI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phases /<br>Sequences | Non-contrast<br>Late arterial<br>Portal venous | T1, T2, PDFF<br>MRCP<br>T1 C+ cholangiography if hepatobiliary agent avialable |
| Pros and cons | &uarr; spatial resolution<br>Cannot evaluate biliary tree | &darr; spatial resolution<br>Better parenchymal and biliary evaluation |

### Findings in parenchyma

- Lesions
    - adenomas: contraindication
- Steatosis
    - NCCT attenuation &le; 40 HU
    - Liver-spleen difference &le; -10 HU
    - Hepatic attenuation index < 0.8
    - Ideal is proton density fat fraction

### Arterial patterns

- Michel's classification

    1. Standard
    2. Replaced LHA from LGA
    3. Replaced RHA from SMA
        - most common variant
        - hepatic artery posterior and lateral to portal vein
        - hepatic artery posterior to biliary duct in hepatoduodenal ligament
    4. Replaced RHA from SMA and LHA from LGA
    5. Accessory LHA from LGA
        - second most common variant
    6. Accessory RHA
    7. Accessory LHA and RHA
    8. One replaced, other accessory
    9. CHA from SMA
    10. CHA from LGA

### Portal venous patterns

- Most common variant: posterior right portal vein arising from main portal vein

### Hepatic venous patterns

- Most common variant: accessory inferior right hepatic vein draining segment VI directly into the IVC
    - &ge; 4 mm needs reimplantation to IVC; smaller can be ligated
    - &gt; 4 cm from RHV will need separate clamping

### Biliary tree patterns

- Most common variant: right posterior sectoral duct draining into left hepatic duct
- Second most common variant: trifurcation

## Liver transplant complications

Hepatic artery RI in post-transplant period:

| ↑ RI (> 0.8) | ↓ RI (< 0.5) |
| --- | --- |
| Transient rise: preserved diastolic flow, resolves by 4 days | Hepatic artery stenosis |
| Impending thrombosis (flow absent in thrombosis) | Thrombosis with collaterals |
| Splenic steal | Coeliac artery stenosis | 
| Graft rejection or dysfunction | Arterioportal fistula |
