## Portal hypertension

### Portal pressure measurement


### Definitions

Baveno VII (2021)

| HVPG (mmHg) | Terminology |
| --- | --- |
| &le; 5 | Normal portal pressure |
| &gt; 5 | Portal hypertension |
| &ge; 10 | Clinically significant portal hypertension (also: if any manifestation is present) |
| &lt; 12 | Target for TIPS in variceal bleeding (also: &darr; of PPG by 50%) |

## TIPS

### Indications

1. Recurrent variceal hemorrhage
    - despite standard first-line combination therapy
2. Recurrent or refractory ascites 
    - requiring ≥ 3 large-volume paracenteses within the past year
3. Pre-emptive TIPS in active variceal bleed within 72 hours
    - Child Pugh C
    - Child Pugh B + active bleeding at UGIE
    - HVPG ≥20 mmHg
4. Rescue / salvage TIPSS 
    - rebleeding despite optimal treatment
5. Budd Chiari syndrome

### Contraindications

| Absolute | Relative |
| --- | --- |
| Severe heart failure |Acute on chronic liver failure |
| Pulmonary artery pressure > 45 mmHg |MELD score &ge; 18 |
| Sepsis |hyperbilirubinaemia |
| Severe HE |Overt hepatic encephalopathy |
