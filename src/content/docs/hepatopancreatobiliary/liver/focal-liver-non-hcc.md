- Atoll sign: peripheral dilated sinusoids in inflammatory adenoma
    - USG: hypoechoic rim around echogenic mass
    - T2: &uarr; around mass
    - Portal venous phase: Peripheral enhancement around mass showing washout

### Complications

- Haemorrhage
    - Highest symptomatic in: SHH type > &beta;-catenin exon 7/8 type
    - On histology, highest in &beta;-catenin exon 7/8 type > SHH type
        - Lowest in HNF-1&alpha; type
        - Inflammatory subtype earlier considered highest based on 2006 classification system
    - Emergency transarterial embolisation
- Malignant transformation
    - &beta;-catenin exon 3 subtype
    - male sex
    - size &gt; 5 cm
    - chronic liver disease (vascular disorders, glycogen storage disorders)

### Management

- Male sex: resection
- Female sex: lifestyle modification and repeat imaging at 6 months, 1 year
    - If significant decrease in size &rarr; annual imaging
    - If size &uarr; 5 cm or increase &rarr; resection
- Transarterial embolisation &rarr; second-line management
    - Ablation: investigational
- Pregnancy: USG every 6-12 weeks
