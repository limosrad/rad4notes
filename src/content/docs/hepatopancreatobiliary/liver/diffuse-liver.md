## Liver trauma

AAST liver injury trauma grading:

| Grade | Haematoma | Laceration depth | Vascular |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | SC < 10% | < 1 cm | - |
| II | SC 10-50%, IP < 10 cm | 1-3 cm | - |
| III | SC > 50%/ruptured, IP > 10 cm | > 3 cm | AB within parenchyma |
| IV | - | 25-75% of a lobe | AB into peritoneum |
| V | - | > 75% of a lobe | Juxtahepatic (IVC/HV) |

(SC = subcapsular haematoma, IP = intraparenchymal haematoma, AB = active bleed)
