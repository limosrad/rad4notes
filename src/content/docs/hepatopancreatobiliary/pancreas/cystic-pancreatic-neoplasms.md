## Summary of cystic pancreatic neoplasms

:::differentials
| Feature | SCA | MCN | SPEN | Cystic PNET | IPMN |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gender | F > M | Almost exclusively F | F > M | F = M | F = M |
| Age | 5th-7th | 4th-5th | 2nd-4th | 5th-6th | 6th-7th |
| Location | Head > body and tail | Body and tail | Body and tail | Body and tail | BD: all regions |
| Morphology | Classic: microcystic or honeycomb appearance, lobulated | Few large cysts, cyst within cyst, thick wall with or without septa | Cystic &plusmn; solid, hemorrhage or necrosis, oval, large (~ 9 cm at presentation) | Cystic and solid (degeneration &plusmn; necrosis) | BD: dilated side branch<br>MD: segmental or diffuse dilatation of MPD |
| Cyst size | < 2 cm | > 2 cm | Variable | Variable | Variable |
| Number of cysts | > 6 | < 6 | Unilocular, thick capsule | Unilocular, thick enhancing wall | BD: variable |
| CT | Multicystic, lobulated, enhancing septa, central calcific scar | Few cysts with occasional mural nodule &plusms; septa | Heterogeneous solid &plusmn; cystic lesion, hyperattenuating areas from haemorrhage | Cystic with smooth margins, hyper vascular peripheral rim | BD: cysts communicating with MPD<br>MD: segmental / diffuse dilatation of MPD |
| MRI | T1 &darr; T2 &uarr;<br>Central scar | T1 &darr; T2: &uarr;<br>Enhancing walls, septa, nodules | T1, T2 variable due to internal haemorrhage and necrosis | T1 &darr; T2 &uarr;<br>Hyper enhancing peripheral rim | T1 &darr; T2 &uarr;<br>Enhancing nodules increase the likelihood of malignancy |
| Calcification | 30-40% in central scar | 25%: wall or septal | 30%: peripheral and punctate | &plusmn; | &plusmn; due to mucin |
| MPD communication | No | No | No | No | Yes |
| Molecular analysis | VHL+ | KRAS, RNF3 +, GNAS - | CTNNB1+ | MEN1, DAXX, ATRX + | KRAS, GNAS, RNF43+ |
| Fluid analysis | ↑ VEGF, ↓ amylase, ↓ CEA | Mucinous, ↑ CEA, amylase variable | ↓ CEA, ↓ amylase | ↓ CEA, ↓ amylase | Mucinous, ↑ CEA, ↑ amylase |
| Management | No follow up if asymptomatic, Sx if space occupying symptoms | Always resected (Kyoto 2024) | Always resected (~ 15% malignant) | Functional: resect<br>Non-functional: observe if <1-2 cm, resect if > 2 cm (NCCN) | BD: According to Kyoto 2024 guidelines<br>MD: always resected |
:::

:::trivia
Kyoto guidelines for branch duct IPMNs:
- Surgery in high-risk: solid component >5mm, MPD ≥ 10mm, OJ
- EUS: cysts with worrisome features \[≥3 cm, solid <5mm, MPD 5-9mm, growth rate ≥2.5 mm/y, wall thickening, nodes, new diabetes, pancreatitis, etc\]
    - inconclusive → surveillance alternating MRI and EUS q 3-6 months
    - < 3 cm: surveillance with EUS (2-3 cm) or MRI (6 months)
:::
