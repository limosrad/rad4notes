## Gall bladder carcinoma

### Pathology

- Adenocarcinoma is most common histology
- Sites: fundus (60%) or body (30%)

### Imaging features

- Three characteristic imaging phenotypes:
    - mass lesions replacing the GB
    - focal or diffuse thickening of the GB wall
    - intraluminal mass in the GB 


### Staging

- Stage I: T1N0M0
    - extension into lamina propria (IA) or muscularis (IB)
- Stage II: T2N0M0
    - extension into perimuscular fibrous tissue
- Stage III: T1-3N1M0, T3N0M0
    - Nodal disease 1-3 nodes, spread to serosa, liver or other organs
- Stage IV: M1, N2 or T4 disease
    - T4: vascular (hepatic artery, portal vein) or &ge; 2 extrahepatic organ involvement
    - N2: &ge; 4 nodes
    - M1: distant metastasis

### Management

- Stage IA: Simple cholecystectomy
- Stage IB: Consider extended cholecystectomy
- Stage II: Extended (radical) choelcystectomy (adjacent liver resection, lymph node sampling)
- Stage III: NACT &plusmn; surgery
- Stage IV: Palliative
