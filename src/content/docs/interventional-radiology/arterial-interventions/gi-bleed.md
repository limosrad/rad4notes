## Imaging guidelines for GI bleed

### Terminology

- Upper GI bleeding: proximal to the ligament of Treitz
- Lower GI bleeding: distal to the ligament of Treitz
- Small bowel bleeding: distal to the ampulla of Vater and proximal to the ileocecal valve
- Suspected small bowel bleeding: no bleeding source is identified after both UGIE and lower endoscopy
- Obscure GI bleeding: no bleeding source is identified after imaging and endoscopy
    - Overt if hematemesis, hematochezia, or melena
    - Occult if not clinically visible but present with positive fecal occult blood test result or iron deficiency anemia (other causes excluded)
- Massive GI bleeding: hemodynamic instability or massive transfusion protocol

### Causes

| Upper GI bleed | Lower GI bleed |
| --- | --- | 
| Peptic ulcer disease / gastritis (most common) | Diverticulosis |
| Varices | Ischaemic colitis |
| Mallory-Weiss tear | Haemorrhoids |
| Neoplasm | Colorectal carcinoma |
| Vascular lesions<br>- angioectasia<br>- Dieulafoy lesion<br>- gastric antral vascular ectasia | Ulcers<br>- infective (TB)<br>- inflammatory (IBD)<br>- stercoral |
| Portal hypertensive gastropathy | Ulcers  |

### Technique sensitivity

- DSA: 0.5-1 mL/min
- CT angiography: 0.1-0.5 mL/min
- <sup>99m</sup>Tc-labelled RBC scan: 0.05-0.1 mL/min

### Indications for imaging in upper GI bleed

- Medical management with UGIE is 1st line

| CTA | DSA |
| --- | --- |
| Bleed on UGIE, unable to see source | Source of bleed seen on UGIE, unable to treat |
| UGIE unavailable, not possible | Recurrent bleed after UGIE, not possible to treat by UGIE |

### Indications for imaging in lower GI bleed

| CTA | DSA |
| --- | --- |
| 1st line in haemodynamically unstable | Unstable with active extravasation on CTA |
| 1st line in stable with suspected active bleed | Not indicated with negative CTA |
| Not indicated in stable without active bleed | Prophylactic embolisation not recommended |

### Indications for imaging in middle GI bleed

- CTE
    - after negative endoscopy &plusmn; capsule endoscopy
    - 1st line if capsule retention is a possibility
    - 1st line if suspected small bowel neoplasm

### Protocols

| Technical Parameter | CTA | CTE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oral contrast | None | Neutral contrast |
| Phases | Non-contrast or virtual non-contrast<br>Late arterial<br>Portal venous phase | Enteric phase<br>&plusmn; arterial in older patients with suspected vascular lesions |

## Visceral artery aneurysms and pseudoaneurysms

### Indications for treatment

- All symptomatic
- All pseudoaneurysms
- Asymptomatic aneurysms in
    
    | All | > 2 cm |
    | --- | --- |
    | Gastric | Splenic |
    | Gastroepiploic | Renal |
    | Gastroduodenal | Hepatic |
    | Pancreatoduodenal | Coeliac |
    | Mesenteric | Jejunal |
    | Colic | Ileal |

- Size increase > 0.5 cm/year
- Women of childbearing age
