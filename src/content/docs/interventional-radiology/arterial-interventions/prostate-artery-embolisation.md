## Indications

- 2026 AUA guideline now recommends PAE as a Conditional Recommendation with Grade B evidence for prostates ≥50 mL


## Contraindications

- Relative
    - bladder diverticula &gt; 2 cm
    - bladder stone
    - detrusor hyperactivity or hypocontractility
    - neurogenic bladder
    - severe renal insufficiency

- PSA &gt; 4 ng/mL warrants MDT for biopsy

## Pre-procedure

- CT angiography / MR prostate + angiography of the pelvis

- De Assis classification of prostatic artery origin

    | Type | Inferior vesical artery origin from |
    | --- | --- |
    | I | Common trunk with superior vesical artery |
    | II | Directly from anterior division of IIA |
    | III | Obturator artery |
    | IV | Internal pudendal artery |


## Procedure

- Internal iliac artery anterior division branch selected
    - PROVISO: largest branches (internal pudendal, middle rectal, obturator, inferior and superior vesicals)
    - Ipsilateral 45 degree oblique view is commonly used to separate them
- PERFECTED method: proximal embolisation first, then embolisa distal
    - Proximal embolisation of the inferior vesical artery
    - Distal embolisation of the prostate parenchyma branches
- Embolic material
    - Diluted microspheres (300-500 &micro;m)
    - Diluted PVA (100-300 &micro;m)
