## Neurointervention

| Trial name | Groups compared | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DAWN<br>2018 | Large vessel occlusion (6-24 hours) with clinical mismatch with and without mechanical thrombectomy (MT) | MT showed improved 90-day outcome compared to medical management alone |
| DEFUSE3<br>2018 | Large vessel occlusion (6-16 hours) with CT perfusion mismatch, with and without mechanical thrombectomy (MT) | MT showed improved 90-day outcome compared to medical management alone |
| ESCAPE<br>2015 | Large vessel occlusion (< 12 hours) with good collaterals on imaging, with and without mechanical thrombectomy (MT) | MT showed improved 90-day outcome compared to medical management alone |
| MR-CLEAN<br>2015 | Large vessel occlusion (< 6 hours) with good collaterals on imaging, with and without mechanical thrombectomy (MT) | MT showed improved 90-day outcome compared to medical management alone |
| ANGEL-ASPECT<br>2023 | Large core infarcts (ASPECT 3-5, 70-100 mL, < 24 hours) with and without MT | MT showed improved 90-day outcome compared to medical management alone |
| SELECT2<br>2023 | Large core infarcts (ASPECT 3-5, 70-100 mL, < 24 hours) with and without MT | MT showed improved 90-day outcome compared to medical management alone |
| ISAT<br>2002 | Ruptured intracranial aneurysm with coiling vs clipping | Coiling &darr; morbidity/mortaility at 1 year and beyond, with small &uarr; risk of rebleeding with coiling |
| ARUBA<br>2014 | Unruptured brain AVMs with medical alone vs medical + surgical, stereotactic or IR management | Medical management alone produced fewer deaths or strokes over mean follow-up of nearly 3 years |
| NASCET<br>1991 | Recent non-disabling stroke with 70-99% carotid artery stenosis managed with medical vs endartectomy | Endartectomy produced fewer strokes and deaths compared to medicam management alone |
| SAMMPRIS<br>2011 | Recent non-disabling stroke with 70-99% intracranial arterial stenosis managed with medical vs Wingspan stent device | Aggressive medical management produced fewer strokes compared to intracranial stenting |

## Arterial intervention

| Trial name | Groups compared | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EMMY trial<br>2016 | Uterine artery embolisation vs hysterectomy | UAE showed lower periprocedural complications and avoided hysterectomy in 2/3rd patients over a 10-year follow-up |
| FEMME trial<br>2021 | Uterine fibroid embolisation vs myomectomy | UFE showed lower improvement in quality of life compared to myomectomy |
| PRECISION V<br>2011 | Conventional TACE vs DEB-TACE | At 6 months, lower adverse effects in DEB-TACE compared to conventional TACE, higher response did not reach statistical significance |


## Venous intervention

| Trial name | Groups compared | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAVENT<br>2012 | Iliofemoral DVT (< 21 days); anticoagulation alove vs catheter directed therapy (CDT) | Lower incidence of post-thrombotic syndrome in CDT group, with marginally higher bleeding risk |
| ATTRACT<br>2017 | All proximal DVT; anticoagulation alone vs catheter directed therapy (CDT) | No difference in the development of post-thrombotic syndrome at 2 years, severity &darr; in CDT group |
