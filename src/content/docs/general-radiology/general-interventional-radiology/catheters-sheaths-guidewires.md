## Catheters

- Types: nonselective, selective, microcatheters

### Catheter parameters

- Diameter = outer diameter in Fr
- Inner diameter = maximum caliber guidewire in "
    - 3 Fr catheter can accomodate 0.018" wire
    - 5 Fr catheter can accomodate 0.035" wire
- Flow rates
    - 3 Fr catheter is 6-8 mL/s
    - 5 Fr catheter is 20-25 mL/s
- Catheter length = hub to tip in cm
    - Commonly used 65 cm and 100 cm
    - Guidewire length ~ 2 times catheter length (rule of thumb)

### Properties of an ideal catheter

- Torqueable: transmission of rotation from hub to tip
    - Important for tortuous vessel navigation
- Memory: retention of shape in vessel lumen
- Pushable: transmission of translation from hub to tip
- Trackable: able to navigate tortuous vessels
- Crossable: able to pass through a target lesion

### Catheter materials

- PTFE: most commonly used material
    - Stiff &rarr; &uarr; pushability, crossability; &darr; trackablility 
- Polyethylene: less stiff
    - &darr; pushability; &uarr; trackablility
- Nylon: highest trackability

### Catheters in neurointervention

- DMSO compatible microcatheters:
    - EVOH (Onyx) degrades ordinary polymers (as it has DMSO)
    - Examples: Marathon, Echelon, Rebar, Apollo
    - Sonic
    - Headway Duo
    - Scepter dual-lumen balloon
