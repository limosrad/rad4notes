## Stress fractures

| Low-risk stress fractures | High-risk stress fractures |
| --- | --- | 
| Compressive forces | Tension forces or poor vascularity |
| Usually managed conservatively with continued weight bearing | May need extended rehabilitation with protected weight bearing &plusmn; surgery<br>due to higher risk of delayed or non-union |
| **Examples** | **Examples** |
| posteromedial tibia | superolateral femoral neck or any >50% width # |
| calcaneus | patella |
| third and fourth metatarsals | anterior tibial cortex |
| medial femoral neck | medial malleolus |

|  | any displaced fracture |

## Pathologic fracture

- Examples:
    - subtrochanteric femur (avulsion of lesser trochanter is pathologic unless proven otherwise)
    - junction of the humeral head and metaphysis
    - vertebral bodies

- Mirel's score for pathologic fracture risk

    | Score criteria | Location | Pain | Lesion matrix | Lesion size |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | 1 | Upper extremity | Mild | Blastic | < 1/3rd |
    | 2 | Lower extremity | Moderate | Mixed | 1/3rd-2/3rd |
    | 3 | Peritrochanteric femur | Functional | Lytic | >2/3rd |

- Prophylactic fixation is requires for score &ge; 9, while score of 8 requires clinical judgement
