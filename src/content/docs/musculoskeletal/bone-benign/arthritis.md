## Other arthitides

### Neurotrophic arthropathy
[mnemonic: Six Ds]
- Distension, joint (earliest ﬁnding, owing to effusion) 
- Density (increased subchondral bone sclerosis) 
- Debris (bony intra-articular fragments)
- Dislocation (joint surfaces often malaligned) 
- Disorganization (joint components usually disrupted “bag of bones”)
- Destruction (articular bone shows loss of bone substance)
