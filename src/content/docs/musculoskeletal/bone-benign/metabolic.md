## Osteoporosis

### Definitions

- Compared to a young healthy reference mean: T-score
- Compared to an age, sex, and ethnicity matched mean: Z-score

| Classification | T-score |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | -1 or greater |
| Osteopenia | -1 to -2.5 |
| Osteoporosis | Less than -2.5 |
| Severe osteoporosis | Less than -2.5 + fragility fracture |

### Indications for a dual energy x-ray absorptiometry

- US guidelines (varies by organisation)
    - All women &ge; 65 years old
    - Postmenopausal women with fracture risk (e.g., FRAX score)
- UK guidelines (NICE/NOGG)
    - in &ge; 30 years old
        - a previous hip or vertebral fragility fracture
        - a single major osteoporotic fragility fracture in the last 2 years
        - 2 or more fragility fractures.
    - Consider in people with a 10‑year major osteoporotic fracture risk of &ge;10%
