## Bone tumour staging

### Enneking / MSK tumour society (MSTS)

| | Low grade | High grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intracompartmental | IA | IIA |
| Extracompartmental | IB | IIB |
| Metastatic | III | III |

### TNM staging for extremities, skull and face

| Stage | Characteristics |
| --- | --- |
| IA | Low grade, &le; 8 cm (T1) |
| IB | Low grade, &gt; 8 cm (T2) or skip lesions (T3) |
| IIA | High grade, T1 |
| IIB | High grade, T2 |
| III | High grade, T3 |
| IVA | Node -, metastasis to lung |
| IVB | Node + or other metastasis |

### T staging for spine and pelvis

- Spine: 5 segments (right and left vertebral bodies, right and left pedicles + transverse processes, and posterior elements)
    - T1: &le; 2 adjacent segments
    - T2: 3 adjacent segments
    - T3
    - T4: spinal canal or vascular involvement
