## Characterising bone lesions

### Approach to solitary bone lesions

![Solitary bone lesions differentials](/images/flowcharts/bone-tumour-approach.svg)

Abbreviations for this page:
- Narr: narrow zone of transition
- Wide: wide zone of transition
- CMF: chondromyxoid fibroma
- LSMFT: liposclerosing myxofibrous tumour
- NOF / FCD: Non ossifying fibroma, fibrous cortical defect
- BFH: Benign fibrous histiocytoma
- OFD: Osteofibrous dysplasia

### Location of the lesion

:::differentials
Classic locations of some bone lesions:
| Epiphysis | Metaphysis | Diaphysis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant cell tumour<br>- reaches till subarticular surface and metaphysis | Simple bone cyst<br>Aneurysmal bone cyst | Langerhans cell histiocytosis<br>- diaphyseal > others |
| Chondroblastoma | Osteosarcoma | Fibrous dysplasia |
| Low grade chondrosarcoma | Chondrosarcoma | Ewing sarcoma |
|  | NOF / FCD | Adamantinoma (anterior tibia) |
:::

- Eccentric and nonexpansile lesions may appear central and expansile if large or within a small/thin bone.

### Zone of transition and sclerotic margins

:::trivia
- Bone lesions with sclerotic margins
    - Aneurysmal bone cyst, simple bone cysts, NOF/FCD, CMF
- Bone lesions without sclerotic margins
    - Giant cell tumours, plasmacytoma, Brown tumours
:::

### Matrix mineralisation

| Matrix type | Seen in |
| --- | --- |
| Absent | Metastasis, Ewing sarcoma |
| Ground glass | Fibrous dysplasia |
| Osteoid (dense ivory, fluffy cloudlike, amorphous) | Osteoid tumours (osteosarcoma) |
| Chondroid (flocculent, comma-shaped, popcorn, rings and arcs) | Enchondroma, chondrosarcoma |

### Periosteal reaction

- Types of periosteal reaction:

| Non aggressive|Aggressive|
| --- | --- |
|- thin<br>- solid<br>- irregular thickened<br>- shell | - parallel spiculated (‘hair on end’)<br>- divergent spiculated (‘sunburst’)<br>- multi lamellated (‘onion skin’)<br>- interrupted (‘Codman triangle’) |


:::differentials
- Aggressive periosteal reactions

| Parallel spiculated (‘hair on end’) |Multi lamellated |Divergent spiculated (‘sunray’)| Codman’s triangle |
| --- | --- |---|---|
|- Ewings sarcoma<br>- Infantile cortical hyperostosis (Caffey’s)<br>- Syphilis | - osteosarcoma<br>- Ewings sarcoma<br>- Langerhans cell histiocytosis<br>- osteomyelitis |- Osteosarcoma<br>- Metastases (especially colorectal)<br>- Ewing sarcoma<br>- Haemangioma (flat bones: skull, pelvis)<br>- Meningioma—skull only | - osteosarcoma<br>- Ewings sarcoma<br>- osteomyelitis |
:::

- Bilateral periosteal reactions: systemic diseases

| Symmetric | Asymmetric |
| --- | --- |
| - hypertrophic osteoarthropathy<br>- pachydermoperiostosis (primary HOA)<br>- thyroid acropachy<br>- fluorosis<br>- vascular insufficiency (varicose veins) | - metastasis<br>- psoriatic and reactive arthritis<br>- non-accidental injury<br>- osteomalacia<br>- sickle cell dactylitis |


### Bone tumour mimics

|  | Age (years) | Location | Centrality | Density | Margin | Expansile | Periosteal Reactionᵇ | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Haemophilic pseudotumour | >10 | Any | Any | Lucent | Narr±Scl | ++ | − | Male ± signs of haemophilia ± pseudotrabeculations |
| Brown tumour | >40 | Any | Any | Lucent | Narr | + | − | Look for other features of hyperparathyroidism |
| Bone island | >20 | Any | Any | Scl | Narr | − | − | Round/oval ± spicules, uniformly dense, <2 cm |
| Bone infarct | Any | Any | Central | Scl+ Lucent | Narr±Scl | − | − | Serpiginous sclerosis, can mimic enchondroma |
| Paget’s disease | >40 | All | All | Lucent/Scl | Narr | − | − | Appearance depends on stage and location |

- Bone infarcts in the epiphysis &rarr; avascular necrosis
:::trivia
- Most common sarcoma arising from a bone infarct: Undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma
- Most common sarcoma arising from Paget's disease: osteosarcoma
:::

### Syndromes with bone tumours

:::trivia
| Syndrome | Bone tumours |
| --- | --- |
| Jaffe-Campanacci syndrome | NOFs |
| McCune-Albright syndrome | Fibrous dysplasias |
| Mazabraud syndrome | Fibrous dysplasias with intramuscular myxomas |
| Ollier disease | Enchondromas
| Maffucci syndrome | Enchondromas + vascular malformations (phleboliths on radiograph) |
| Cystic angiomatosis | Haemangiomas |
:::

- Polyostotic forms often occur in atypical locations and have atypical appearances.

## Lucent bone lesions without overt aggressive features

### Commoner bone tumours

|  | Age (years) | Location | Centrality | Density | Margin | Expansile | Periosteal Reactionᵇ | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Simple bone cyst | 5–20 | Met±Dia | Central | Lucent | Narr+Scl | −/+ | − | Unilocular. ‘Fallen fragment’ if fractured |
| Aneurysmal bone cyst | 5–30 | Met | Eccentric | Lucent | Narr+Scl | ++ | − | Expansile, bubbly. May occur 2° to another lesion |
| Giant cell tumour | 20–50 | Met+Epi | Eccentric | Lucent | Narr/Wide | + | − | Nonsclerotic margin, often abuts articular surface |
| NOF/FCD | 5–25 | Met | Cortical | Lucent | Narr+Scl | −/+ | − | NOF >3 cm; FCD <3 cm. Sclerose after healing |
| Osteoid osteoma | 10–30 | Dia/Met | Cortical | Lucent± Ost | Scl | − | Solid | Lucent nidus <2 cm + surrounding sclerosis |
| Osteoblastoma | 10–30 | Dia/Met | Eccentric | Lucent± Ost | Narr±Scl | + | Solid | Lucent nidus >2 cm; spine>flat bones>long bones>talus |
| Chondroblastoma | 5–20 | Epi | Eccentric | Lucent± Chon | Narr+Scl | − | −/Solid | Classically epiphyseal; also in apophyses, patella, tarsals |
| Intraosseous lipoma | >20 | Met | Any | Lucent± Scl | Narr±Scl | −/+ | − | Lucent ± central sclerosis. Calcaneus>prox femur>other |
| Fibrous dysplasia | >3 | Dia | Central | Ground glass | Narr±Scl | + | − | Variable density. Commonest in long bones, ribs, skull |
