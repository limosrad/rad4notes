## Osteoid Osteoma

### Differentials

:::differentials

| | Osteoid osteoma | Brodie's abscess |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Size | < 2 cm | > 1-2 cm |
| Inner margin | Regular | Uneven |
| Nidus | Regular and central | Sequestrum &rarr; irregular and eccentric |
| Nidus enhancement | +++<br>Early arterial enhancement<br>Spares mineralised parts | - |
| Penumbra sign | - | + <br>T1 &uarr; halo around lesion |

:::
