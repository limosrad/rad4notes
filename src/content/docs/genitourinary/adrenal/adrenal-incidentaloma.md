## ESE/ENSAT guidelines (2023)

### True incidentaloma

- Size &ge; 1 cm in a patient without symptoms of adrenal excess or other malignancy
    - First imaging workup: NCCT adrenals
- Biochemical workup with 1-mg overnight dexamethasone supression test for all inciddentaloma
    - Steroid profiling if imaging features suggestive of adrenocortical carcinoma
- Biochemistry for phaeochromocytoma for all > 10 HU lesions.

| Attenuation | Size < 4 cm | Size &ge; 4 cm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| &le; 10 HU | No follow up | No follow up |
| 10-20 HU | Additional imaging now OR<br>NCCT/MRI follow up at 12 months | MDT&plusmn;further imaging<br>Surgery or 6-12 month imaging follow-up |
| &ge; 20 HU | MDT&plusmn;further imaging<br>Surgery or 6-12 month imaging follow-up | Surgery after malignancy work-up |
| Heterogenous | MDT&plusmn;further imaging<br>Surgery or 6-12 month imaging follow-up | Surgery after malignancy work-up |

- On follow-up imaging
    - &uarr; by &ge; 5 mm or &gt; 20% &rarr; consider surgery
    - &uarr; but below this threshold &rarr; repeat imaging at 6-12 months

### Known case of extra-adrenal malignancy

- If result of pathological assessment change management of primary &rarr; biopsy &plusmn; PET/CT or resection
- If result does not change management of primary &rarr; manage and follo-up with imaging per perimary malignancy

## ACR White Paper (2017)

- Recommends adrenal washout CT in 1-4 cm lesions with attenuation > 10 HU
    - Do at 12 months for 1-2 cm, and immediately for 2-4 cm
        - No enhancement or washout positive: benign
    - Adrenal washout CT has been shown to be much poorer after this publication
