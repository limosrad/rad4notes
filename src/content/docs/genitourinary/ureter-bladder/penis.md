## Priapism
Management (EAU 2026, AUA 2022):

### Definition
- Prolonged erection for > 4 hours  

### Differentials
:::
Ischaemic vs non-ischaemic priapism
| Diagnosis | Ischaemic (95%) | Non-ischaemic (5%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| History | Painful, rigid erection | Perineal or penile trauma; painless, fluctuating erection |
| Penile Doppler (after ABG [AUA]) | Sluggish or non-existent blood flow | Normal arterial flow and may show turbulent flow at the site of a fistula |
| Cavernosal ABG (after Doppler [EAU]) | Dark blood; hypoxia, hypercapnia and acidosis | Bright red blood; arterial blood gas values |
| Management | Emergency | Elective |
|  | Aspiration, irrigation, intra-cavernosal phenylephrine | Pudendal angiography ± embolisation |
:::
