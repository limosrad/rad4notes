## Imaging workup

- Cystoscopy – choice for tissue diagnosis
- MRI for T-staging
    - VI-RADS: standardised reporting to predict muscle-invasive bladder cancer
- CT urography
    - for high-risk TCC (e.g., in trigone), co-existent upper tract TCC, nodal and distant metastasis (add CT chest)
    - Can also detect high local grades (perivesical fat [T3b], visceral [T4a], or pelvic side wall [T4b] invasion)
