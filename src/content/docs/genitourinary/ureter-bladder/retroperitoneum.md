## Retroperitoneal fibrosis

:::differentials
Retroperitoneal fibrosis vs retroperitoneal malignancy

|  | Retroperitoneal fibrosis | Retroperitoneal malignancy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aorta | No anterior displacement of aorta | Anterior displacement of aorta and IVC |
| Extent | Caudal location, up to the sacral promontory | Closer to infrarenal aorta |
| Margins | Irregular margins | Lobulated margins |
| Relationships | Infiltrative relationship, enveloping structures | Invasive relationship, with mass effect on adjacent structures |
| Signal | T2 ↑ or ↓ signal based on disease activity | T2↑, if T2 ↓ more likely to be benign fibrotic RPF |
| Bone and muscle | No bone destruction or muscle invasion | Occasional bone destruction and muscle invasion |
:::
