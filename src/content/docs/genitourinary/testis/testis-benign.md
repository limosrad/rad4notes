## Torsion of the Testis
:::differentials
Acute testicular / scrotal pain

| Finding | Torsion | Epididymo-orchitis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scrotal wall oedema | - | + |
| Vascularity | &darr; | &uarr; |
| Epididymal thickening | - | + |
| Vascular pedicle | Twisted | No twist |

- Transient hyperaemia in testis after detorsion &rarr; due to revascularisation.

:::

## Trauma to the Testis
:::differentials
Testicular trauma

| Finding | Fracture | Rupture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tunica albuginea | Intact | Disrupted |
| Contour of testis | Smooth | Interrupted |
| Parenchyma | Disrupted | May be extruded into a hematocele |
| Management | Conservative unless ischaemia | Surgery |
:::
