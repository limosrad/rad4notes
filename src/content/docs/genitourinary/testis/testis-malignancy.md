## Tumour markers 

(Abd Radiology 2019)

:::differentials
|  | Seminoma | NSGCTs | Teratoma |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AFP | Normal | ↑↑ (Yolk sac, embryonal) | Normal in pure teratomas* |
| b-HCG | 20% | ↑↑(Choriocarcinoma, embryonal) | Normal |
| Adjuvant choice | Radiosensitive |  | Chemosensitive |

\*AFP ↑ in mucinous or hepatoid differentiated teratomas

:::

## Imaging workup for testicular cancer

- Bilateral testicular ultrasound – suspected
- Perform contrast CT CAP (MRI if contrast allergy) – in diagnosed TC
- Brain MRI – multiple lung metastases, ↑ β-HCG values, poor-prognosis risk group.
- No PET or bone scan for staging.
