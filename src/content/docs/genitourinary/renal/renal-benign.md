## Cystic renal disease

### Differential diagnosis of adult renal cysts

| ADPKD | Enlarged kidneys | Liver, seminal vesicle cysts, berry aneurysms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VHL | RCCs | Pancreatic cysts, NETs<br>Haemangioblastomas |
| Uremic | Small kidneys, regress after transplant | ↑ RCC risk |
| Lithium | 2-5 mm microcysts | Past history of bipolar |

### Bosniak classification for renal cysts

| Class | Findings | Management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Low attenuation, no septa or calcifications | Nothing |
| II | - anything else - | Nothing |
| IIF | Any wall or septa 3 mm thick≥ 4 thin septa<br>Heterogenous T1 FS hyperintensity | Re image 6 monthly x 1 year then annually for 4 years (total 5 years) |
| III | Thick (≥ 4 mm) or nodular enhancing walls or septa | Urology consult |
| IV | Enhancing nodule (i.e., acute angles with wall/septa) | Urology consult |

:::trivia

| Grade | Parenchyma | Collecting system | Vascular |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | subcapsular haematoma (<3.5 cm)/ contusion |  |  |
