## Complications of renal transplant

:::differentials
1.      Urologic

| Haematoma | Urinoma | Abscess | Lymphocoele |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| < 1 week | ~10 days | Weeks-months | 2 weeks-6 months |
| Complex collection | Clear collection | Complex collection | Simple ± septa |
| CT: ↑ density | CT: contrast leak | CT: rim enhancement | CT: ↓ attenuation, no leak, ± septa |
| MRI: T1 ↑ | Nuclear: tracer leak | USG: rim hyperaemia | Intervention: sclerotherapy |

2.      Nephrology (biopsy – best, MAG3/DTPA – 2nd best)

|  | Acute rejection | Acute tubular necrosis | Cyclosporine | Chronic rejection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanism | Immune mediated | Delayed ischemia | Nephrotoxicity | T-cell mediated |
| Time | 1 week-months | < 1 week | Months | Months |
| Perfusion = blood flow | ↓↓ | =/↓ | =/↓ | ↓↓ |
| Uptake = filtration | ↓↓ | =/↓ | ↓↓ | ↓↓ |
| Excretion = transit | ↓↓ | ↓↓ | ↓↓ | ↓↓ |

3.      Vascular

| Compartment syndrome | Renal artery thrombosis | Renal vein thrombosis | Renal artery stenosis (most common) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| < 2 hours | Hours | 1-5 days | 3 months-2 years |
| Absent cortical colour | Absent flow | Reversal in arterial flow | PSV > 200 cm/s<br>Renal artery aorta ratio > 2 |
| Adjacent collection | Wedge-shaped infarcts | Thrombus in vein<br>Enlarged kidney | Stenosis at surgical anastomosis |
:::
