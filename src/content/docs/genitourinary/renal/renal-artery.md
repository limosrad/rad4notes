### USG findings in renal artery stenosis

| Direct signs | Indirect signs |
| --- | --- |
| Main renal artery | Segmental arteries |
| PSV &ge; 200 cm/s | Acceleration time > 70 ms |
| PSV ratio (renal artery:aorta) &ge; 3.5 | Acceleration index < 300 cm/s<sup>2</sup> |


### Differetials of renal artery stenosis

:::differentials

| Feature | Atherosclerosis | Fibromuscular dysplasia |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | >66%, most common cause | 2nd most common cause |
| Age | Older adults (>45 years) | Middle aged adults (F:M = 9:1) |
| Location | Proximal artery | Mid and distal artery |
| Bilaterality | 30% | 66% |
| Management | Stent placement > angioplasty | Angioplasty only |

:::
