## Medical Disclaimer

Rad4Notes is intended solely as an educational and revision resource for medical students, radiology trainees and healthcare professionals.

The material presented here is not a substitute for standard medical textbooks, peer-reviewed literature, clinical guidelines, formal teaching or professional training. Although care is taken to maintain accuracy, the content may contain errors, omissions or outdated information.

**The material must not be used as the basis for clinical decisions or patient management. It should not influence patient care.** Clinical decisions must always be based on appropriate clinical assessment, authoritative and up-to-date references, institutional protocols and the judgement of qualified healthcare professionals.

Readers are responsible for independently verifying information before applying it in an academic or professional context.
