## Spectrum of infections in HIV

Depends on the CD4 count.

:::differentials
Infections in HIV
| CD4 count | Common infections |
| --- | --- |
| &gt; 200 | TB, bacterial (most commonly pneumococcal) |
| &lt; 200 | **PCP**, NTM |
| &lt; 100 | CMV, disseminated fungal and NTM |
:::

NTM includes *M. kansasii* (which looks similar to TB on imaging) and *M. avium* complex (which has distinctive radiology features).

### Imaging characteristics of some infections

:::differentials
Imaging findings for a few infections in HIV
| Infection | Radiology findings |
| --- | --- |
| PCP | bilateral perihilar GGOs, pneumatoceles &plusmn; pneumothorax<br>nodes and effusions are unlikely |
| MAC | bronchiectasis + centrilobular nodules, effusion and nodes are rare<br>mainly affects RML and lingula |
| Kaposi | nodular or reticular consolidation in a perihilar distribution (“flame-shaped”)<br>hyperattenuating nodes, significant pleural effusions |

:::

## Spectrum of infections in bone marrow transplant recipients

### Stages of bone marrow transplantation

:::differentials
Bone marrow transplant recipients
| Stage | Days after transplant | Common pathologies |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-engraftment | &lt; 30 | Infectious: fungal<br>Non-infectious: oedema, DAH, engraftment syndrome, drug toxicity |
| Early post-engraftment | 30-100 | Infectious: CMV, PCP<br>Non-infectious: idiopathic pneumonia syndrome, acute radiation pneumonitis |
| Late post-engraftment | &gt; 100 | Chronic GvHD: BO, OP |
:::

- Idiopathic pneumonia syndrome is a diagnosis of exclusion.

## Spectrum of infections in lung transplant recipients

:::differentials
Lung transplant recipients
| Timing | Differentials |
| --- | --- |
| &lt; 1 week | Reperfusion (GGOs with septal thickening)<br>Air leak |
| 1 week - 2 months | Acute rejection (GGOs with septal thickening)<br>Bronchial leaks |
| &gt; 2 months | CMV infection (rare before 2 weeks)<br>Bronchial stenosis |
| &gt; 4 months | Chronic rejection (BO and OP)<br>PTLD (< 1 year)<br>Recurrence (common in sarcoidosis) |
:::
