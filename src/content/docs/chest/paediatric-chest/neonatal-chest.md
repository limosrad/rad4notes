## Summary of neonatal chest disorders

:::differentials
Summary of common neonatal chest x-ray patterns:

| Normal volume or hyperinflated | Low volume |
| --- | --- |
| **TTN**: post LSCS or diabetic mother, fluid in fissures, cardiomegaly, resolves in 2-3 days<br>**MAS**: postmature, asymmetric patchy ‘ropy’ consolidation + peripheral ↑inflation, complication: pneumothorax, P-HTN, PDA | **RDS**: preterm, < 6 hours, symmetric granular opacities<br>**GBBHS** pneumonia: non-specific, pleural effusions |
:::
