## Mediastinal compartments and contents

- On radiographs: superior, anterior, middle, posterior compartments
- On CT and MRI: prevascular, visceral and paravertebral compartments

## Differential diagnosis of prevascular / anterior mediastinal masses

| Solid masses | Cystic masses |
| --- | --- |
| Thymoma | Thymic cyst |
| Lymphoma | Lymphatic malformation |
| Germ cell tumour | Cystic teratoma |
| Retrosternal goitre | Retrosternal colloid goitre |
| | Pericardial cyst |

### Solid prevascular / anterior mediastinal masses

:::differentials
4Ts of solid prevascular mediastinal masses:
| Differential | Demographic | Imaging morphology | Additional features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thymic neoplasms | Middle aged (> 40 years) | Off midline lobulated mass<br>Calcifications are rare and indicate &uarr; risk type | Pleural drop metastasis in &uarr; risk<br>Myasthenia, hypogammaglobunemia or pure red cell aplasia |
| Germ cell tumours | Children and young adults | Large midline mass<br>Calcification and fat in teratomas<br>Heterogenous in NSGCTs | Elevated tumour markers<br>Lung metastasis in seminomas and NSGCTs |
| Lymphoma (HL>NHL) | Most common mediastinal mass<br>Adult patients with bimodal distribution in HL | Bulky homogenous rounded masses<br>Compression of solid organs with infiltration late<br>Patent vessels coursing through masses | B symptoms present<br>Other nodal enlargement may be seen | 
:::
