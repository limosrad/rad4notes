## Hamartoma

:::trivia
Pulmonary hamartomas:
- Presence of macroscopic fat is virtually diagnostic.
- Practically a benign lesion, hence usually left alone.
:::

## Carcinoids

:::differentials
Typical and atypical carcinoids
| Typical carcinoids | Atypical carcinoids |
| --- | --- |
| Central airways | Peripheral airways |
| Usually benign | &uarr; risk of malignancy |
:::


### Typical imaging findings
- Avidly enhancing endobronchial mass with collapse of distal bronchopulmonary segment
    - Much of the lesion may be obscured by the collapse &rarr; iceberg lesion
- Calcification is common in the mass.

### DIPNECH
- nodules + mosaic attenuation &plusmn; carcinoid syndrome

## Solitary fibrous tumour of the pleura

### Imaging features
- Large pleural based mass (often &gt; 10 cm
- Forms obtuse angles to the chest wall
- Change in position with slight movement is characteristic
    - Due to pedunculation
- T2 &darr; due to fibrous component
- usually benign
    - aggressive forms show local spread (unlike mesotheliomas)


:::trivia
Associations of SFT of the pleura
- Dodge-Potter syndrome: hypoglycaemic episodes
- Hypertrophic osteoarthropathy: painful periosteal thickening
:::
